**Rules that make bios work harder:**

- Lead with what you *make*, not what you *are*. "I make 30-second weeknight
  recipes" beats "food enthusiast & storyteller."
- Put searchable keywords in: brand managers search "Tokyo food creator", not
  your name. Category + city + format is the searchable triple.
- Include your email in the bio itself wherever the platform allows.
- Drop the emoji-bulleted list of adjectives. It reads as filler to anyone
  reviewing applications at volume.
