# Canned answers

Application forms ask the same nine questions forever. Write each answer *once*,
well, then paste. This file is the actual time-saver in this toolkit — the pitch
templates get used a few times a week; this gets used every single application.

Keep each answer inside the character limits noted. Replace the bracketed parts.

---

### "Tell us about yourself" (short — ~150 chars)

> [Name] — [one_liner from creator-profile.yml]. [Location], [languages].

### "Tell us about yourself" (long — ~500 chars)

> I'm [Name], and I make [content type] for [audience]. [One sentence on why you
> started — a real reason, not a mission statement.] My audience is mostly
> [top countries, age range], and they come to me for [the specific job your
> content does for them]. [Differentiator sentence.]

### "Why do you want to work with this brand?"

Do **not** answer "I love your brand." Answer with a fact only a real user or
close observer would know:

> I've been using [specific product] since [when] — [specific, true detail about
> how you actually use it]. The thing I'd want to show my audience is [angle],
> because [reason tied to what your audience asks you about].

If you have genuinely never used the product, say what drew you to it instead.
Never fabricate a usage history — brands ask follow-up questions.

### "What's your content idea for this campaign?"

The highest-leverage box on the form. Most applicants write nothing specific here,
so anything concrete wins.

> [Format + length]: [the actual concept in one sentence]. Opening frame is
> [hook]. I'd shoot it [setting/style] to match how the rest of my feed looks.
> [One line on why this concept suits *your* audience specifically.]

### "What are your rates?"

> My standard rates are [X] for a TikTok video and [Y] for an IG Reel, including
> 30-day organic usage. Happy to quote a package for multiple deliverables, or to
> price to your budget if you tell me the range and the usage you need.

### "Which brands have you worked with?"

> [Brand] — [deliverable], [result]. [Brand] — [deliverable], [result].

No past partnerships yet? Do not leave it blank and do not invent any:

> No paid partnerships yet — I've been focused on growing organically first. My
> closest comparable is [link], where I featured [product/category] and it did
> [result]. I'm looking for a first brand partner in [category].

### "What's your turnaround time?"

> Draft within [N] days of receiving product, one round of revisions, posting on
> the date you specify. If you need something faster, tell me the deadline and
> I'll tell you honestly whether I can hit it.

### "Do you have usage rights available?"

> Organic usage on your channels for 30 days is included. Paid amplification,
> whitelisting, and perpetual usage are available as separate line items — tell
> me which you need and the window and I'll quote it.

### "Anything else we should know?"

Use it. Most people leave it empty. One line — the strongest fact you have not
already said:

> [e.g. "My audience is 62% Japan-based, so if you're launching in the JP market
> this reaches a segment most English-language creators can't."]
