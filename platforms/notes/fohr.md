# FOHR

- **URL:** https://www.fohr.co
- **Category:** 3 — discovery-weighted
- **Priority: LOW effort, but do the setup properly.**

## How creators get work

Primarily a brand-side discovery and campaign-management tool. Creators build a
profile and connect analytics; brands search and invite. Fohr has historically
put real weight on audience-quality/authenticity scoring, so connected analytics
and a clean audience matter more here than raw follower count.

**Do not spend recurring time here.** One good setup pass, then leave it.

## What it asks for

- Connected social accounts (this is the whole point — it verifies you)
- Complete bio, categories, location
- Portfolio / previous work

## Working it

- Keywords in your profile are what surface you in brand searches. Category +
  city + format.
- Make sure your email is visible in your profile so an interested brand can
  reach you without going through the platform.
- If a brand invites you through Fohr, that's a warm lead — reply with
  `templates/rate-negotiation.md`, not a cold pitch.
