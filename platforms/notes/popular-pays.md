# Popular Pays

- **URL:** https://popularpays.com
- **Category:** 2 — hybrid; matched more than applied
- **Priority: MEDIUM — complete the profile, then let it work.**

## How creators get work

Part of Lightricks. Creators build a profile and are matched to brand campaigns;
there is a creator-side app for managing collaborations. Historically strong on
content production for brands, so expect a meaningful share of briefs to be
content-licensing rather than post-on-your-channel.

## What it asks for

- Portfolio — weighted more heavily here than raw follower count
- Connected channels and audience data
- Content categories and style
- Rates, including content-only (no posting) — set `rates.ugc_no_posting`

## Working it

- Your portfolio is doing the selling. Put your best *production* work up, not
  just your highest-view posts.
- Read each brief for whether you're posting or just delivering assets, and price
  accordingly. They're different products at different rates.
