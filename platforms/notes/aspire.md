# Aspire

- **URL:** https://www.aspire.io
- **Category:** 1 — open creator marketplace
- **Priority: HIGHEST.** One of only two on your list with browsable campaigns.

## How creators get work

Brands run campaigns on a marketplace you can browse and apply to directly.
Formerly AspireIQ. Skews toward established DTC/ecommerce brands, which generally
means real budgets rather than gifted-only.

## What the application asks for

- Connected social accounts — it reads real analytics, so don't inflate anything
- Audience demographics (geo, age, gender)
- Free-text pitch → `templates/campaign-application.md`
- Your rate, or acceptance of the campaign's fixed fee
- Portfolio pieces / prior brand work

## Working it

- Profile completeness affects what you get shown. Fill every field once.
- Apply to campaigns the day they appear — early applications get read when the
  reviewer is still reading carefully.
- Put a real content idea in the pitch box. It's the only field where you compete.
