# IZEA

- **URL:** https://izea.com
- **Category:** 1 — marketplace with opportunity listings
- **Priority: HIGH.** Your second real application channel.

## How creators get work

Long-running public company in this space. Creators build a profile and respond
to opportunity listings / casting calls, alongside direct brand bookings. Product
naming has changed repeatedly over the years (IZEAx, Flex, Creator Marketplace) —
check what the current creator-side dashboard calls it.

## What the application asks for

- Profile with connected channels and audience data
- Per-channel rates — IZEA leans more on listed rates than most, so set these
  deliberately using `rates/rate-card.md`
- Content categories/topics — these feed brand search, choose them carefully
- Response to the specific opportunity brief

## Working it

- Listed rates do real work here. Too low and you anchor yourself down; too high
  with a thin profile and you never surface.
- Check listings on a schedule. Volume matters more than perfection.
