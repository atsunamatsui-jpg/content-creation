## Corrected from an earlier note

This was logged as "The Criqle" and flagged unverified — that was a misspelling.
The real platform is **The Cirqle**, and it's genuinely documented: an
Amsterdam-based influencer marketing platform.

## How creators get work

The Cirqle's core product is turning creator content into **paid ads**
(whitelisting) rather than running a browsable campaign board — closer in
shape to Popular Pays or Social Native AI than to Aspire. As of November 2025
it has a **direct integration with Meta's Creator Marketplace**, so a listing
may surface there rather than through a separate application on
thecirqle.com. The company relaunched as "Cirqle V2" in 2026.

**Genuinely unresolved:** the exact creator-side signup flow outside the Meta
integration isn't publicly documented. Check thecirqle.com directly, or watch
for an invite via Meta's Creator Marketplace, rather than assuming a form
shape in advance.

## What it likely asks for

- Connected social accounts
- Content/portfolio samples
- Whitelisting and usage-rights terms — this is the actual commercial
  product here, so expect it to be a real negotiated field, not boilerplate

## Working it

- **Price whitelisting separately from a post fee.** Running your content as
  paid media is a different product than an organic post, and should be
  priced to the spend level and duration, not folded into your standard rate.
- If you're not seeing anything through thecirqle.com directly, check whether
  a brand has reached you through Meta's Creator Marketplace instead — that
  may now be the more active channel.
