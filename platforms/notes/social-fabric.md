## Corrected from an earlier note

Previously logged as "Inmar Intelligence" — that's the parent company.
**Social Fabric** is the actual creator-facing brand: Inmar's community,
built on its 2016 acquisition of Collective Bias. Use this name and URL when
applying; "Inmar Intelligence" won't get you to the right signup page.

## How creators get work

Creators join the Social Fabric community and receive campaign invitations —
often CPG, grocery and retail brands, frequently tied to in-store or
shopper-marketing activations. Community size has been reported anywhere
from 8,000 to 10,000+ shopping-focused creators depending on the source and
year — treat any specific figure as approximate.

Worth knowing: the retail/CPG angle is a genuinely different brand pool from
the DTC brands on Aspire. Low overlap, so it costs you little to be in both.

## What joining asks for

- Channels, audience data, demographics
- Household/shopper information is plausible here given the retail focus —
  more than a typical influencer platform asks. Decide what you're
  comfortable sharing.
- Content categories

## Working it

- Campaign invites tend to be structured, with specific deliverables and
  retailer tie-ins. Read the requirements closely — these briefs are usually
  more prescriptive than DTC campaigns.
- If your content includes snack/food/grocery-adjacent material, that's a
  genuinely strong fit for this platform's brand pool specifically — lead
  with it rather than a generic lifestyle pitch.
