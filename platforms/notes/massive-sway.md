# Massive Sway

- **URL:** https://www.massivesway.com
- **Category:** 3 — network; join once, then inbound
- **Priority: LOW effort — one application to join, then email.**

## How creators get work

The influencer network operated by Sway Group. You apply to join the *network*
once; after that, campaign opportunities come to you, historically by email, and
you opt in to the ones that fit.

There is no campaign board to work. The single action that matters is joining and
then actually reading the emails.

## What joining asks for

- Channel links and audience stats
- Content categories
- Sometimes a rate range

## Working it

- **Filter these emails into a folder you actually check.** Network opportunities
  are usually first-come and time-limited — the creators who win them are the
  ones who reply fast, not the ones with the best pitch.
- Log opted-in campaigns in the tracker like any other application.
