# Inmar Intelligence

- **URL:** https://www.inmar.com
- **Category:** 3 — community; join once, then invited
- **Priority: LOW effort.**

## How creators get work

Large retail-data and promotions company whose influencer arm grew out of
acquiring Collective Bias (the Social Fabric creator community). Creators join
the community and receive campaign invitations — often CPG, grocery and retail
brands, frequently tied to in-store or shopper-marketing activations.

Worth knowing: the retail/CPG angle is a genuinely different brand pool from the
DTC brands on Aspire. Low overlap, so it costs you little to be in both.

## What joining asks for

- Channels, audience data, demographics
- Household/shopper information is plausible here given the retail focus — more
  than a typical influencer platform asks. Decide what you're comfortable sharing.
- Content categories

## Working it

- Campaign invites tend to be structured, with specific deliverables and
  retailer tie-ins. Read the requirements closely — these briefs are usually more
  prescriptive than DTC campaigns.
- Verify the current creator-side entry point; the branding has shifted over the
  years since the Collective Bias acquisition.
