# Social Native

- **URL:** https://www.socialnative.com
- **Category:** 2 — hybrid, UGC-weighted
- **Priority: MEDIUM.**

## How creators get work

Content-creation platform for brands (acquired Olapic). Creators opt in and
receive/apply to briefs. Weighted toward UGC and content production — briefs are
often "produce assets we'll use in our ads" rather than "post to your audience."

## What it asks for

- Portfolio and content samples
- Channels and audience data
- Content-production rates

## Working it

- **Price on the UGC line, not the influencer line**, unless a brief explicitly
  includes posting to your channels. Use `rates.ugc_no_posting`.
- Ads usage is the norm here, not the exception — read the usage window and
  channel scope on every brief. Perpetual all-channel usage should cost more.
- UGC work doesn't fatigue your audience, so it's a reasonable volume play
  alongside your posting deals.
