## The pattern in your list — read this first

**Only three of your nine take proactive applications.** The rest are
join-the-network-then-get-invited platforms, or brand-side tools where you appear
because a brand searched for you.

That changes what you should actually do. Sending pitch after pitch into the
other six is effort with nowhere to land. For those, the win condition is a
complete, analytics-connected, keyword-rich profile — then you wait to be found.

| Platform | Can you apply? | What actually works |
|---|---|---|
| **Aspire** | ✅ Open campaigns | Volume applications. Your main board. |
| **IZEA** | ✅ Opportunities / casting calls | Check listings regularly; apply fast. |
| **Impact** | ✅ Per-brand programs | Apply to individual brand programs. Affiliate, not campaign work. |
| **Popular Pays** | 🟡 Hybrid | Complete profile; opportunities are largely matched to you. |
| **Social Native** | 🟡 Hybrid, UGC-weighted | Expect content-licensing briefs more than posting deals. |
| **FOHR** | 🟡 Discovery-weighted | Profile completeness + connected analytics is ~all of it. |
| **Massive Sway** | ❌ Join, then inbound | Apply to the network once, then watch email. |
| **Inmar Intelligence** | ❌ Community, then inbound | Join, complete profile, wait for campaign invites. |
| **The Criqle** | ❓ Verify | Smallest/least-documented. Sign up, see what the dashboard offers. |

## What this means for your week

1. **Aspire and IZEA get your application time.** They are the only two on this
   list with browsable campaigns you can pitch into at volume. Use
   `templates/campaign-application.md` here.
2. **The six inbound platforms get a one-time setup pass**, not recurring effort.
   Complete every profile field, connect analytics, put searchable keywords in
   (category + city + format — brand managers search "Tokyo food creator", not
   your name). Then stop touching them.
3. **Impact is a different job entirely** — affiliate income on content nobody
   paid you for. Set it up once, apply to relevant brand programs, let it run.
4. **Your list has a gap: open campaign boards.** With only two applyable
   platforms, your application volume is capped by what Aspire and IZEA happen to
   post. Worth adding two or three from `platform-guide.md` Category 1 —
   Collabstr, Creator.co, Afluencer and Insense all have genuinely open boards.
5. **Inbound platforms make the cold pitch more valuable, not less.** When six of
   nine platforms won't let you apply, going direct to brands with
   `templates/cold-pitch-email.md` stops being optional.
