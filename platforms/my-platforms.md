## The pattern in your list — read this first

**Only three of your nine take proactive applications.** The rest are
join-the-network-then-get-invited platforms, brand-side discovery tools where
you appear because a brand searched for you, or — The Cirqle specifically —
a performance/whitelisting platform that may surface a listing through Meta's
Creator Marketplace rather than its own application form.

That changes what you should actually do. Sending pitch after pitch into the
other six is effort with nowhere to land. For those, the win condition is a
complete, analytics-connected, keyword-rich profile — then you wait to be found.

| Platform | Can you apply? | What actually works |
|---|---|---|
| **Aspire** | ✅ Open campaigns | Volume applications. Your main board. |
| **IZEA** | ✅ Opportunities / casting calls | Check listings regularly; apply fast. |
| **Impact** | ✅ Per-brand programs | Apply to individual brand programs. Affiliate, not campaign work. |
| **Popular Pays** | 🟡 Hybrid | Complete profile; opportunities are largely matched to you. |
| **Social Native AI** | 🟡 Hybrid, UGC-weighted | Now predictive-AI matching; expect content-licensing briefs more than posting deals. |
| **The Cirqle** | 🟡 Hybrid, whitelisting-focused | Check Meta's Creator Marketplace for a listing; price usage rights separately from a post fee. |
| **FOHR** | 🟡 Discovery-weighted | Profile completeness + connected analytics is ~all of it. |
| **Massive Sway** | ❌ Join, then inbound | Apply to the network once, then watch email. |
| **Social Fabric** | ❌ Community, then inbound | Join at socialfabric.us, complete profile, wait for campaign invites. |

## What this means for your week

1. **Aspire, IZEA, and Impact get your application time.** They're the three on
   this list you can genuinely apply into at volume. Use
   `templates/campaign-application.md` for Aspire/IZEA; Impact is a separate
   per-program affiliate flow.
2. **The Cirqle is worth a lighter touch now that it's verified** — check Meta's
   Creator Marketplace for an existing listing before assuming a standalone
   application exists, and price any whitelisting ask separately from a
   standard post fee.
3. **The five remaining inbound platforms get a one-time setup pass**, not
   recurring effort. Complete every profile field, connect analytics, put
   searchable keywords in (category + city + format). Then stop touching them.
4. **Impact is a different job entirely** — affiliate income on content nobody
   paid you for. Set it up once, apply to relevant brand programs, let it run.
5. **Your list has a gap: open campaign boards.** With only three fully-applyable
   platforms, your application volume is capped by what Aspire, IZEA, and Impact
   happen to post. Worth adding two or three from `platform-guide.md` Category 1
   — Collabstr, Creator.co, Afluencer and Insense all have genuinely open boards.
6. **Inbound platforms make the cold pitch more valuable, not less.** When most
   of these nine won't let you apply directly, going direct to brands with
   `templates/cold-pitch-email.md` stops being optional.
