RATE NEGOTIATION

Rules that hold across almost every negotiation:
  1. Never name the first number if you can avoid it. "What's the budget for
     this?" is a complete, professional reply to "what are your rates?"
  2. When you must go first, quote a package, not a per-post price. Packages
     are harder to line-item down.
  3. Concede scope, never rate. Dropping from 3 deliverables to 2 protects
     your price. Dropping your price resets it permanently for that brand.
  4. Usage rights and exclusivity are separate line items. Brands routinely
     ask for perpetual whitelisting inside an organic-post fee — that is
     often worth more than the post itself.
  5. Silence is fine. Send the number, then stop typing.
-->

**When they ask your rate before naming a budget:**

Happy to put a quote together. What's the budget range you're working with for {{campaign}}, and what usage do you need beyond organic? Those two move the number the most, so I'd rather price it to your actual brief than send a generic card.

**When they say the budget is lower:**

That works — I'd just reshape the scope to match rather than the rate. At {{their_number}} I'd do {{reduced_deliverables}} instead of the full package, same production quality and same usage terms. Want me to write it up that way?

---

**When they ask for perpetual/unlimited usage rights:**

Happy to include that — it's a separate line because it's genuinely a different product. Organic-only for 30 days is included at the quoted rate. Perpetual usage across your paid channels would be {{perpetual_uplift}} on top. If you only need paid rights for a defined campaign window, that's cheaper — tell me the window and I'll price it.
